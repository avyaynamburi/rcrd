BESTOSYS DENTAL SOFTWARE - FULL SCREEN-BY-SCREEN DESCRIPTION
(as used by the clinic DENTALPROFILES, from the two "babai meeting" recordings, 3 Aug 2026)
=====================================================================================

HOW TO READ THIS FILE
---------------------
This describes every part of the BestoSys app that was shown in the two meeting
recordings, in enough detail to draw each screen on paper. Positions are given for
a normal full-size window of 1920 pixels wide by 1080 pixels tall, with x measured
from the left edge and y measured from the top edge. If you draw on an A4 sheet in
landscape, roughly 1 cm on paper = 70 pixels across.

Real patient names, phone numbers and account numbers have been left out on purpose.
Where an example is needed, a placeholder like "Patient" or "#1234" is used.

BestoSys is version v9.64.1. It runs as a Windows desktop app. The clinic has two
branches (the app calls them "centers"): RBI Layout and Bellandur.


=====================================================================================
PART 1 - THE BIG PICTURE: WHAT EVERY SCREEN LOOKS LIKE
=====================================================================================

Every screen in BestoSys is built from the same four pieces. If you draw these four
first, every other screen is just "what goes in the big white area".

1. TITLE BAR (the very top strip, about 22 px tall, full width)
   - Far left: a small purple letter "b" logo, then the word "BestoSys".
   - Far right: the usual Windows minimise, restore and close buttons.

2. ICON RAIL (the left edge, about 70 px wide, the full height of the window)
   - A solid dark navy-blue strip.
   - A tiny green dot sits in its top-left corner (it shows the app is online).
   - A column of white icons, each with a small white word under it. The icon for
     the screen you are on turns orange.
   - The full list of icons is in Part 2.

3. TOP BAR (a white strip about 60 px tall, starting just right of the rail)
   - On the left, at about x = 90, the clinic name in dark-blue capital letters:
     DENTALPROFILES. On a form or a patient's profile, a left-pointing back arrow
     sits just before it.
   - On the right, the contents change depending on the screen:
       * On the Calendar: a search box, a "Compact mode" switch, a center (branch)
         dropdown, a doctor dropdown, a "+ New" button, and the date and time.
       * On almost every other screen: a row of seven small coloured circles with
         numbers in them, then the date and time (for example "3 Aug, 2026 10:07 pm").
         The circles are a quick count of today's appointments: navy = total,
         grey = scheduled, red = checked in, orange = engaged, green = checked out,
         dark grey and light grey = the remaining statuses (light grey is cancelled).

4. WORK AREA (everything else: right of the rail, below the top bar)
   - White background. This is where each page draws its content.
   - When you open Settings, Admin, CRM or the bell, a second white menu (about
     290 px wide) slides out right next to the rail, from about x = 70 to x = 360,
     and the page fills the rest, from about x = 370 to the right edge.

Two things float on top of almost every screen:
   - BESTO AI BOT: a round lavender button in the bottom-right corner (about
     x = 1815, y = 990). To its left there is often a white speech bubble saying
     "I am Besto AI Bot, Please ask me your queries" with a small red x to close it.
   - "Need Help?": an orange link, usually in the bottom-left corner of forms.

At the very bottom of the rail is the version text "v9.64.1".

HOW TO DRAW THE TWO MOST IMPORTANT LAYOUTS

LAYOUT A - THE CALENDAR (the home screen)
   Draw a big rectangle for the window.
   - Left edge: the narrow navy icon rail, full height.
   - Top: the white top bar. Left side "DENTALPROFILES". Right side, left to right:
     a long Search box, the word "Compact" with a small switch, a "Center" dropdown,

     on the right a row of six small outlined buttons (Today, Day, Week, Month,

   - The rest splits into three columns:
       (a) A very thin time ruler on the far left (times every 15 minutes).
       (b) A wide grid of 7 day columns (SUN to SAT), taking about 75% of the width.
           Today's column is shaded light peach and today's date number is inside a
           navy circle. Appointments are pink rectangles inside the columns.
       (c) A "Today" side panel on the far right, about 345 px wide.

LAYOUT B - ANY SETTINGS PAGE (about 40 pages look like this)
   - Left edge: the navy icon rail, with the gear icon highlighted orange.
   - Top bar: "DENTALPROFILES" on the left, the seven status circles and time on the

   - Next to the rail: the white second menu - a column of bold navy group names,
     each with a small down-arrow on its right. One group is open, showing its
     pages underneath in grey, with the open page in orange.
   - The rest of the screen: the page itself. Usually a navy page title at the
     top-left, a filter row under it, buttons at the top-right ("+ New", a green
     Excel icon, a red PDF icon, a blue printer icon), and a table below.

PART 2 - THE LEFT ICON RAIL, ICON BY ICON
=====================================================================================

The rail has two groups of icons: seven near the top and five near the bottom, with
an empty gap between them. Listed top to bottom with their rough height (y) on the
screen:

  1. Dashboard (y about 235) - icon of four small squares in a grid.
     Opens the Dashboard (a summary chart and a "things that need attention" list).

  2. Calendar (y about 295) - icon of a calendar page.
     Opens the appointment calendar. This is the main screen the clinic uses all day.

  3. Patients (y about 355) - icon of a person.
     Opens the patient list.

  4. Reports (y about 415) - icon of lined rows.
     Opens reports. (This was never opened in the recordings.)

  5. Admin (y about 475) - icon of an ID card.
     Opens a second menu with Team, NABH, Accounting, Inventory, Lab, Tasks, SMS/WA.

  6. CRM (y about 535) - icon of a handshake / diamond shape.
     Opens a second menu with Practice Growth, CRM and Communications.

  7. Help (y about 610) - icon of a light bulb.
     Opens Help and Support, with a Support Tickets tab.

  (empty gap from about y = 620 to y = 790)

  8. Notifications (y about 810) - a bell with a small red badge showing "30".
     Opens a small "Alert" menu (Application Update, Recent Features).

  9. WhatsApp (y about 860) - the WhatsApp logo with a small red "New" tag.
     Opens the WhatsApp module (shows whether the clinic's WhatsApp is connected).

 10. Settings (y about 900) - a gear.
     Opens the large Settings second menu (Part 8). This is where most setup lives.

 11. User avatar (y about 940) - a white circle with the logged-in user's initials,
     for example "FD" for the front desk login or "RT" for the owner login.

 12. Logout (y about 985) - an arrow leaving a box.
     Opens a small box: "Are you sure you want to logout?" with No and Yes.

 At the very bottom: the text "v9.64.1".

PART 3 - CALENDAR
=====================================================================================

The Calendar is the screen the app opens on. It has a top bar, a toolbar, the day
grid in the middle and the "Today" panel on the right.

3.1 TOP BAR (y about 50), left to right after "DENTALPROFILES"
   - A grey icon of a group of people (x about 825).
   - A Search box with a magnifying glass on the left and an x on the right
     (x about 850 to 1145). As you type a patient's name, a white panel drops down
     under it listing matches as "Name (patient number)" with a grey line
     "Last Appt: date [doctor name]" and a small calendar icon on the right of
     each result.
   - The words "Compact mode" with an on/off switch beside them.
   - A center (branch) dropdown, showing "Bellandur" or "RBI Layout". Changing it
     reloads the whole calendar with that branch's appointments.
   - An "All Doctors" dropdown. Opening it shows a "Search doctors..." box at the
     top, then "All Doctors", then each doctor's name.
   - A navy "+ New" button (opens the new appointment form, section 3.6).
   - The date and time, for example "3 Aug, 2026 9:19 pm".

3.2 CALENDAR TOOLBAR (y about 110)
   - Left: two small arrows (< and >) to move a week back or forward, then the start
     date as text with a small down-arrow, for example "Sunday Aug, 2 2026 v".
   - Right: a row of outlined buttons: Today, Day, Week, Month, Schedule,
     Doctor / Operatory. The view you are using has a darker outline.

3.3 THE GRID
   - Header row (y about 145 to 175): each column shows the day name in small
     capitals (SUN, MON, TUE, WED, THU, FRI, SAT) with the date number under it.
     Today's number sits inside a filled navy circle, and today's whole column is
     shaded a light peach colour.
   - Time ruler on the left: tiny grey times every 15 minutes, from 12:15 am down
     to late night. The hours after the clinic closes are greyed out.
   - A thin blue line with a dot at its left end runs across today's column at the
     current time.
   - APPOINTMENT CARDS: each appointment is a pink/magenta filled rectangle whose
     height matches the length of the appointment. Inside it, three lines of white
     text:
        line 1 (bold): a small status icon then the patient's name. A clock icon
                       means scheduled, an arrow icon means already checked out.
        line 2 (italic): the doctor's name.
        line 3: start time and length, for example "3:30p 30mins".
     When two appointments overlap, they sit side by side and share the column.
   - WEEK view shows seven columns.
   - DAY view shows only one day, with the appointments stretched across the full
     width.
   - SCHEDULE view turns the grid into a list grouped by date: on the left a navy
     circle with the date number and "AUG, MON" next to it; on the right,
     full-width pink bars, one per appointment, each with name, doctor and time.

3.4 THE APPOINTMENT POP-OVER (click any pink card)
   A white card about 290 px wide and 450 px tall opens beside the card you clicked,
   with a small x in its top-right corner. From top to bottom it shows:
     1. An arrow icon and the patient's name in bold, then gender and age, then the
        patient number (#...).
     2. The phone number, with three small icons under it: copy, a green location
        pin, and a green WhatsApp logo.
     3. A small red label "Payment Due", the amount in large text ("INR 0.00"), a
        credit-card icon on the right (hovering it says "Receive payment"), and an
        underlined link "Bill & Collect".
     4. A box with a coloured left edge holding the doctor's name, the start time
        with the word "today", and the length on the right.
     5. A list of action links: Edit, Missed, Check-In, Cancel Appointment, Copy.
        If the patient has already been checked out, it shows Undo Check-Out and
        Copy instead.

3.5 THE "TODAY" PANEL (the right-hand column, x about 1565 to 1910)
   From top to bottom:
     - A small ">" arrow at the left (collapses the panel) and the word "Today"
       centred.
     - Three tabs: Appointments (underlined when active), Online, Follow ups.
     - Six coloured counter tiles in a row, each a number under a short label:
       navy = total (for example 13), dark grey "Sch" (scheduled), red "In"
       (checked in), orange "Eng" (engaged), green "Out" (checked out), grey
       "Canc" (cancelled). A small circular refresh arrow sits after them.
     - Two collapsed filter rows, each with a down-arrow on the right:
       "Treatment Categories" and "Doctors".
     - A yellow strip with a warning such as "4 patients haven't checked-in yet!"
     - A scrolling list of today's patients. Each row has a coloured strip on its
       left edge, the patient's name in bold, a small clock icon with the time and
       length, a small red box with a "?" (no treatment category chosen yet), an
       undo arrow, and the status word on the right ("Sch" in grey, "In" in red,
       "Out" in green) with a small check-in arrow icon.

3.6 THE "+ NEW" APPOINTMENT FORM
   A large white box (about 1000 x 650 px) opens in the middle of the screen. The
   calendar behind it goes darker. A red x sits in the top-right corner.
   - Top row of tabs: Appointment, Task, Doctor Unavailable.
   - Second row of tabs: Single Appointment, Appointment Series, Group Appointment.
   - Then the form, in rows. Each row has a grey label on the left:
       WHERE   - "Center" dropdown and "Operatory" (room/chair) dropdown, side by
                 side.
       WHO     - "Doctor" dropdown across the full width. Under it, a "Patient"
                 search box. Typing in it shows a list of matching patients as
                 "Name (#number)". Once chosen, the patient's mobile number
                 appears inside the box with a phone icon. A "+ New" button on the
                 right lets you add a brand-new patient.
       PURPOSE - "Treatment Category" dropdown with its own search box. The
                 categories include Consultation, Cosmetic, Crown & Bridge,
                 Dentures, Endodontics, Implants, Not Specified, Occlusal Therapy,
                 Online Appointment, Oral Surgery, Orthodontics, Others,
                 Paedodontics and more. Then a "Treatment" dropdown (also
                 searchable) that only shows treatments from the chosen category
                 (for Cosmetic: Bleaching, Chemical Peel, Diastema Closure and
                 others). Then a Notes box.
       WHEN    - a Date box (calendar icon), a Time box (clock icon) and a
                 "Walk-In" button. Under them a "Duration" dropdown with choices
                 5 min, 10 min, 15 min, 20 min, 30 min, 45 min, 1 hour,
                 1 hour 30 min, 2 hours, 2 hours 30 min, 3 hours, 4 hours,
                 5 hours, 6 hours, Custom.
       NOTIFICATIONS - a collapsed grey row reading "Patient: WA/SMS, Email on
                 Reminder".
   - Bottom row: "Need Help?" in orange on the left; CANCEL (outlined) and SAVE
     (navy) on the right.
   - If you save an appointment outside the doctor's hours, a small box pops up:
     "BestoSys - Doctor is not available on selected date" with a red Close button
     and a "Proceed anyway" button.
   - A successful save shows a green message in the bottom-right corner:
     "Appointment added successfully!" The new appointment appears in the grid and
     in the Today list, and the counters go up by one.

3.7 UPDATE APPOINTMENT (Edit from the pop-over)
   The same form as "+ New", but without the top tabs. It is filled in with the
   appointment's details. A small red link with the other branch's name appears in
   the bottom-left corner, for moving the appointment to that branch.

3.8 BILL & RECEIVE (the "Bill & Collect" link)
   A box in the middle of the screen:
     - A "Doctor" dropdown across the top.
     - "Payment": an Amount box, and round buttons to pick the payment mode: Cash,
       Chq/Online, Card, Wallet, Other.
     - "Notes" box.
     - A small table with columns Treatment, Charges, Tax, Total. A blue + adds a
       row and a red circle-minus removes one.
     - An "Auto Billing" tick box.
     - CANCEL and SAVE buttons.

The Dashboard is two big white cards stacked on top of each other.

   - Top bar, right side: a "Select Center" dropdown and the status circles.

   - CARD 1, "Practice Summary" (top half of the screen):

       * Four tabs: Inventory (underlined), Lab Task, Tasks, Payment Due.
       * Under the tabs, a small "Status: Active" dropdown.
       * The list below it was empty ("Looks like there's nothing to show").

PART 5 - PATIENTS
=====================================================================================

5.1 PATIENT LIST
   - Three tabs at the top-left: RECENTLY ADDED, RECENTLY VISITED, INACTIVE. The
     chosen tab has a navy underline.
   - Top-right: a Search box, the status circles, the time, and a navy "+ New"
     button under them.
   - Each patient is a row about 50 px tall across the whole width:
       * a grey person outline (or their photo) on the left,
       * the name, with the patient number in grey underneath,
       * their phone number(s) in the middle,
       * their email in blue,
       * on the far right, an amount owed in red (for example "Due. 6,000.00") and
         a small calendar icon, when there is one.
   - An empty tab shows the grey message "Looks like there's nothing to show here",
     a red link "Can't find patient? Tap here." and a round navy + button.

5.2 NEW PATIENT FORM ("+ New")
   The form takes over the whole screen. A back arrow is in the top-left corner.
   Six tabs run across the top: BASIC INFO, CONTACT, INSURANCE, MORE, MEDICAL,
   DEMOGRAPHICS. Small < and > arrows on the far left and far right edges move to the
   previous or next tab. Labels sit in a column on the left, and the boxes sit in a
   column about 550 px wide to their right, usually two boxes per row.

   TAB 1 - BASIC INFO
     - A photo box in the top-left (a grey person outline with a small blue pencil
       badge to add a photo).
     - Center (required) - a dropdown across the full width.
     - Title dropdown + First/Full Name (required)  |  Surname
     - Middle Names  |  Suffix
     - Gender (required): round buttons Male, Female, Transgender, Unknown  |
       Date Of Birth (required): round buttons "DOB" (with a date box) or "Age"
       (with a number box).
     - Marital Status dropdown.
     - CONTACT: +91 dropdown + WhatsApp Number  |  +91 dropdown + Other Mobile;
       Personal Email  |  Work/Other Email.
     - IDENTIFICATION: Nationality dropdown  |  National ID (Aadhar Card Number);
       ID Type dropdown.
     - RELATIONSHIP: Patient Since (a ticked "New" box, a date, and a red x)  |
       Group dropdown; Ratecard (required, normally "Standard"); a Search Tag box.
     - SOURCE: Source Type dropdown  |  Source dropdown. The Source Type list
       includes Ref by Patient, Ref by Internal Drs/Staff, Ref by External
       Drs/Other, Website, Facebook, Google, WhatsApp, Email, SMS, Instagram, Shared
       via Mobile, Practo.com, JustDial, Pamphlets and more.
     - NOTES: one wide text box.

   TAB 2 - CONTACT
     The form slides to the left and a new pane appears on the right with:
     - a "Paste from center" outlined button (copies the clinic's address),
     - ADDRESS: Address line 1, Locality, State dropdown, Area Code, Landmark,
     - OTHER CONTACTS: Emergency contact name, Spouse Name, General Practitioner
       dropdown.

   TAB 3 - INSURANCE
     "Primary Insurance" with: Company dropdown, Sub Company dropdown, Policy
     dropdown, Insurance Id Number box, and a "+ Add Another Insurance" link.

   TAB 4 - MORE
     - Family (empty area).
     - Notification preference: tick boxes "Standard Appointment Reminder" (ticked),
       "SMS DND", "Email DND" (DND = do not disturb).
     - Patient Tracker: an "Excluded" tick box and an "Exclude Note" box.
     - Patient Portal Access: on/off switches for Show Treatments (on), Allow to Edit
       Profile, Show Treatments Charges, Allow to Edit Medical Conditions, Show Files
       (on).

   TAB 5 - MEDICAL
     Four labelled rows, each a box with clickable grey "chips" (small tags) under it:
     - Medical Conditions - chips: Diabetes, Blood Pressure, Thyroid, Epilepsy, Heart
       Disease, Asthma, Dental History, Acidity, Cholesterol, Kidney Disease,
       Pregnant/Breast Feeding, on Antiplatelet therapy, on Steroid therapy.
     - Dental Conditions - chips: Bad Breath, Bleeding gums, Sensitivity, Discoloured
       teeth, Stained teeth, Fractured teeth, Missing teeth, Cracked teeth, and six
       "Pain in the ... teeth" chips (lower right back, lower left back, upper right
       back, upper left back, upper front, lower front).
     - Current Medications - a text box.
     - Allergies - an "Allergic to" box and a "Select Allergic to Drugs" box.
     - A collapsed "Habits" row with a down-arrow on the far right.

   TAB 6 - DEMOGRAPHICS
     - Demographics: Language (English), Religion, Occupation, Ethnic Group and Race
       dropdowns, stacked.
     - Other: Blood Group dropdown and DISC Profile dropdown, side by side.

PART 6 - A PATIENT'S PROFILE PAGE
=====================================================================================

You get here by opening a patient from search, the list or the calendar pop-over.
The page has a narrow left column (about 300 px) of small cards, and a wide right
area. The rail stays on the far left, and the top bar shows a back arrow,
DENTALPROFILES and the status circles.

6.1 LEFT COLUMN (x about 10 to 310), top to bottom
   1. Identity card: a round circle with the patient's two initials, the name in
      bold, "Gender, age (date of birth)", the patient number with the branch in
      brackets (for example "#1234 [RBI Layout]"), a phone line and an email line
      each with a small icon, then a translate icon and the language ("English").
   2. A row of about ten small grey icons (calendar, kit, headset, documents,
      clipboard, expand, two printers, link, pencil).
   3. Three buttons in a row: "SMS/WA" with a +, "Email", "Letter" with a +.
   4. Relationship card: the word "Relationship", the date they became a patient,
      and a small (i) icon. Under it two columns: Group / Ratecard (for example
      None / Standard) and Membership / Family. Then "Reviews" with a Google "G"
      icon and an Instagram icon.
   5. Social Notes card: the title, a navy + and a collapse arrow on the right, a
      small cartoon of two people sitting at a table, and the caption "Know your
      patient better. Nurture a bond."
   6. Internal Notes card: same look, caption "Know your patient's preferences.
      Serve them better."
   7. An "Address" row with a down-arrow (collapsed).
   8. A green word "Active" with a blue "Change" link.

6.2 RIGHT AREA - TOP STRIP
   - Left: "Medical Information [date]" with a pencil, and four small rows under it:
     History, Dental, Allergies, Habits.
   - Middle: "Blood Group"; and "Contact" with a pencil, showing Gen. Practitioner
     and Emergency contact.
   - Far right, in a box: "Payment Due", the INR amount, and three links: Receive,
     Refund, Send Payment Link.

6.3 TABS
   Four tabs spread evenly across the right area: VISITS (navy underline when
   active), DOCS & IMAGES, MEMBERSHIP, CHARTING.

6.4 VISIT HISTORY (under the VISITS tab)
   Each past visit is a block that starts with an orange date tag and a small print
   icon. Under it, up to four sections, each starting with a small coloured dot:
   - CLINICAL NOTE: a table heading row (#, Surface, Pop, TOP, Pulp Sensibility,
     Conclusion, Files), then "Chief Complaint" and "Findings" as labelled lines of
     text. Teeth are written using FDI numbers (for example 18 = upper right third
     molar).
   - TREATMENT DONE: rows with a light green background. Columns: Treatment (with
     the tooth number), Charges, Gross Amt, Disc, Net Amt, GST, Total, Bill (the
     bill number as a link), Files. A Total row at the bottom and a print icon on
     the right.
   - PRESCRIPTION: columns Drug, Dosage (Morning, Noon, Night), Duration, Total Qty,
     Instruction. A WhatsApp-share icon and a print icon on the right.
   - RECEIPTS, REFUNDS, CREDIT NOTES: columns Voucher #, Mode, Doctor.
   Under the visits: "Expand latest visit only" and a "Get Lifetime Data" button.
   A patient with no recent visits instead shows "No data in last six months.
   Please click on Get lifetime data to see the past data" with two buttons:
   "Get Lifetime Data" and "Add first Visit".
   A round navy + button floats in the bottom-right corner (next to the AI bot) to
   add a new visit.

6.5 NEW VISIT FORM (the floating + button)
   The form appears at the top of the VISITS tab:
   - Row 1: Center dropdown, Visit Date (with a red x to clear), Doctor dropdown,
     Appointment dropdown (links the visit to today's appointment), and a "Check-In"
     button on the far right.
   - Row 2: a blue link "Update Medical History, Habits, Allergies" on the left and a
     navy "Change Doctor" button on the right.
   - Then six grey bars, each with a + on its far right. Clicking one opens that
     section:
        Clinical Note, Treatment Plan, Treatment Done, Package, Prescriptions,
        Instructions.
   - At the bottom: "Need Help?" on the left, CANCEL and SAVE.

   CLINICAL NOTE (opened)
   A bordered box with a small red bin icon in its top-right corner:
   - Doctor dropdown across the full width.
   - A two-column grid of boxes:
        Teeth                 |  Surface (dropdown)
        Pain On Palpation (dd)|  TOP (dropdown)
        Pulp Sensitivity (dd) |  Conclusion (dropdown)
   - Clicking "Teeth" drops down a TOOTH PICKER - a chart of small numbered squares
     laid out like a mouth:
        * a box labelled UR in the top-left corner, UL in the top-right, LR in the
          bottom-left, LL in the bottom-right;
        * top line: baby teeth 55 54 53 52 51 | 61 62 63 64 65
        * next line: adult upper teeth 18 17 16 15 14 13 12 11 | 21 22 23 24 25 26
          27 28
        * next line: adult lower teeth 48 47 46 45 44 43 42 41 | 31 32 33 34 35 36
          37 38
        * bottom line: baby teeth 85 84 83 82 81 | 71 72 73 74 75
        * a "Full Mouth" button under the chart.
   - A "Vitals" row with a down-arrow.
   - Chief Complaint box, with a blue "Pain Scale" link at its right end.
   - Findings, Investigation, Diagnosis and Notes boxes, one under another.
   - A helper column on the right side (x about 1525 to 1885): an "Options" dropdown
     (set to Complaints), a Search box, and a list of ready-made phrases - Pain,
     Swelling, Bleeding Gums, Sensitivity, Discolouration, Ulcer - that you click to
     fill in the box you are typing in.

   PRESCRIPTIONS (opened)
   - Red column headings: "Drug [+]", "Dosage", "Duration".
   - Each medicine is a row: a small person icon, a searchable drug dropdown, the
     form of the drug (for example Tablet), a round button for regular dosing or
     "SOS" (only when needed), three small boxes for Morning / Noon / Night amounts,
     a number + "Days" dropdown for how long, the total quantity, a red bin to delete
     the row, and a drag handle (two lines) to reorder.
   - A second line under each medicine: a food timing dropdown (before/after food),
     a language dropdown (English), an Instruction box, and a Notes box.


=====================================================================================
PART 7 - (reserved) SHARED BUILDING BLOCKS USED EVERYWHERE
=====================================================================================

About 40 pages use the same layout, so it helps to know it once.

7.1 THE STANDARD LIST PAGE
   1. A navy page title at the top-left of the work area (for example "Center List",
      "Drug Master List").
   2. A filter row under it: small grey labels sitting above dropdowns (usually
      "Center" and "Status = Active"), sometimes a Search box, then a navy "View"
      button and a grey "Reset" button.
   3. At the top-right, in a row: a navy "+ New" button, a green Excel icon, a red
      PDF icon and a blue printer icon.
   4. The table: a thin grey border, small grey capital-letter column headings (some
      with a sort arrow and a funnel filter icon), white rows about 32 px tall.
      "Yes" is written in green and "No" in red.
