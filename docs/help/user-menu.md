# Using your user menu

Select **User** at the bottom of the navy rail to see your name, role and current center. Use **Need Help?** in the menu for these instructions. Press Escape, choose Close or click outside to dismiss it. You can open it with Tab and Enter.

During setup, the avatar shows a dash and the menu says **Not signed in**. No identity or center is assigned. Change password and Switch center remain disabled until clinic sign-in is connected. Once connected, the avatar will use your initials and the center list must include only centers you may access.
