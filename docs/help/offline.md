# When the connection is unavailable

A red connection dot and a warning mean the app server cannot be reached. Changes cannot be saved until it responds again. The app checks every 30 seconds and when your device reconnects.

Keep the current page open. Typed form contents stay in memory during the outage; they are not automatically submitted when you reconnect. Once the warning disappears, review your work and save it.

Do not refresh, close the page or navigate away from an unfinished form. Unsaved work is not stored on your device and cannot survive a reload. If a save fails, keep the form open and retry after the connection returns.
